Evidence before conclusions.

Why did reported malaria indicators change? AfyaEvidence investigates reporting revisions, completeness and testing patterns, then prepares traceable findings for a person to review.

Watch the demonstrationExplore the implementation
Demonstration scope: fictional facilities and synthetic aggregate reports only. AfyaEvidence is not a clinical diagnostic tool or a deployed disease-surveillance system.
The question

An unusual figure needs an explanation, not a guess.

A change in reported positives may reflect expanded testing, a late revision, missing records or an unresolved movement in accepted indicators. AfyaEvidence separates what the reporting evidence explains from what still requires investigation.

Check the records

Inspect the facility register, submission history and report versions. Identify missing, conflicting or withdrawn records.

Explain the figures

Compare raw and accepted totals using deterministic calculations. Ground each proposed explanation in evidence.

Keep people accountable

Deliver a candidate brief marked awaiting human review. No autonomous approval, source editing or outbreak declaration.

How it works

A bounded investigation workflow

The model proposes actions within explicit permissions; host-controlled tools retain authority over source selection, arithmetic and evidence checks.

1. ZetarisReviewed JDBC access to registered synthetic CSV-backed reporting tables
2. ValidationVersion-aware snapshots, reporting quality and consistent comparisons
3. OllamaLocal, bounded model proposals and approved investigation tools
4. H-MEMPinned Meterless reference for scoped historical context where available
5. Human reviewEvidence-linked brief requiring a separate reviewer decision

The successful integrated demonstration recalled zero historical H-MEM notes. Memory integration was exercised, but recalled notes did not influence that explanation.
